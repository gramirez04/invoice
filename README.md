# Professional Notary & Loan Signing Invoice Template

A clean, interactive, and print-ready HTML invoice template designed specifically for Notary Publics and Loan Signing Agents. 

This is a standalone HTML document. It requires no software installation, no monthly subscriptions, and no server hosting. It runs entirely in your web browser and exports beautifully to PDF.

## ✨ Features
* **Pixel-Perfect PDF Export:** Engineered to print cleanly on standard 8.5" x 11" paper with flawless alignment.
* **Custom Branding:** Upload your own square logo directly from your computer (auto-replaces the default template branding).
* **Smart Layout:** Blank address lines and empty fields automatically collapse when printed to keep the layout looking sharp.
* **Auto-Calculations:** Subtotals, discounts, and totals are mathematically locked for perfect alignment.
* **Privacy First:** 100% local processing. Your business data, client details, and financial information never leave your device. 

## 🚀 How to Use It

**Option 1: Use it directly from the web**
1. Visit the live template here: `[Insert your GitHub Pages Link Here]`
2. Fill out your business details, client info, and line items.
3. Click your browser's Print button (or press `Ctrl+P` / `Cmd+P`) and choose **"Save as PDF"**.

**Option 2: Download it to your computer**
1. Click the green **Code** button at the top of this repository and select **Download ZIP**.
2. Extract the ZIP folder.
3. Double-click the `index.html` file to open it in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
4. Fill it out and print to PDF!

## 💡 Pro Tip
Once you enter your primary business information and upload your logo, you can save the file locally to your desktop. Open that saved file next time to use it as a pre-filled master template!
